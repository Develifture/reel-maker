# Measure, diagnose, iterate

The sample showed that views follow audience size and age more than any craft choice. So the only way to learn what works for this user is to test on their own account and compare each reel against their own history, not against the benchmarks below.

## Benchmarks from the 199-reel sample (for orientation only)
| Metric | p25 | Median | p75 | p90 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Views | 19K | 81K | 180K | 491K |
| Likes / views | 1.66% | 2.14% | 2.58% | 2.93% |
| Comments / views | 0.16% | 0.91% | 2.08% | 3.58% |
Comments/views among reels with a keyword offer: median 1.56%. Without: 0.07%.
Median likes/views of the top-quartile reels by views was 2.6%, against 1.7% in the bottom quartile.
These accounts are mostly larger than a new creator's. Use likes/views and comments/views (rates), not raw views.

## What to log for every reel
`date, topic, archetype of hook, hook text, length (s), spoken words, format, keyword (or none), offer type, views at 48h, views at 7 days, likes, comments, shares, saves, follows gained, average watch time (if shown), notes`

Check each reel at the same age (for example 48 hours and 7 days). In the sample, median views kept growing for weeks (about 34K at 14 to 30 days, 112K at 30 to 60 days), partly because older reels in a saved collection are survivors. Never compare a 2-day-old reel with a 2-month-old one.

## Run the test
1. Pick one variable per reel: the hook archetype, the length, the opening visual, or the call-to-action.
2. Keep the topic family constant for a run of 6 to 8 reels, and rotate the variable.
3. Rank reels by views relative to the user's own median at the same age. Look at the top and bottom two, not at single results.
4. Keep what wins twice. Drop what loses twice.

## Diagnose from the numbers (heuristics, not proven rules)
| Pattern | Likely cause | Fix to try |
|---|---|---|
| Low views, likes/views at or above their median | The hook or opening frame did not earn the swipe, or the topic was stale | Rewrite the hook using a different archetype; put the proof in the first frame; check whether the topic is already saturated |
| Fair views, likes/views clearly below their median | The reel did not deliver on the hook | Show the result earlier; cut filler; align the hook with what you actually show |
| Keyword reel, comments/views under about 0.5% | Weak or hidden call-to-action, or an unappealing reward | Put the call-to-action in caption line 1, say it aloud and show it on screen, make the reward specific |
| High views but few follows | The topic travelled but did not signal what the account is about | Add a one-line series framing in the call-to-action or the caption |
| Strong comments, weak views | The funnel works but distribution does not | Test hooks, and post when the topic is fresh |

## Before the next reel
Write one sentence: "I changed X; if it works I should see Y in Z." Then check it.
